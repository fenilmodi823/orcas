Region What it does Measured
1 Identity block Name, what the thing is, and — third line — what frame the data is in. Declaring the coordinate frame in the chrome, permanently, is the single most quietly rigorous decision in the interface. Title ~2.05cqw, tracking 0.32em, weight 400. Subtitle 0.88cqw / 0.26em / 500. Frame line 0.72cqw / 0.2em, dimmed.
2 View modes Three mutually exclusive layers — Navigation, Color, Petrova. Radio behaviour, not toggles. Neutral is the default; colour is something you opt into. Pills with a leading dot, 0.72cqw / 0.18em. Active fills the dot and lifts the border. The fiction layer alone takes the signal colour.
3 Status HUD Four label/value pairs, right-aligned. Range is live camera distance and it is the whole orientation system — it answers "where am I?" continuously in a space with no landmarks. Labels 0.7cqw / 0.15em, dimmed; values 0.78cqw, weight 700, tabular. Unit switches to light-years alone below ~1 pc.
4 Named destinations Four camera presets. Navigation is entirely place-based — there is no coordinate entry and no free-flight mode. Bordered rectangles, 0.72cqw / 0.18em. Active takes the signal colour at ~8% fill.
5 In-scene labels Leader line plus name. Two tiers: signal-coloured for objects on the story path, pale for everything else. Curated — not generated per object. 0.7cqw / 0.15em with a leading dot. No collision test against the fixed chrome — one label renders under the HUD in every capture.
6 Input hint States the two controls that exist, permanently, at the lowest possible contrast. No tutorial, no overlay, no dismissal. 0.68cqw / 0.14em at roughly 25% of body luminance.

The measurement that surprised me. Every string in the DOM is sentence case — Range, 17.72 pc / 57.8 ly, About this chart. The uppercase is entirely text-transform. It costs nothing and it means the copy stays readable and translatable in source while the display stays instrument-like.

What transfers, and what must not

Idea Verdict Why
Four-corner chrome, empty centre Take it. Already ORCAS's direction — Phase 3 decided there is no right-hand object panel and no side rails. This is independent confirmation from someone solving the same problem.
Permanent live range readout Take it. The cheapest orientation device there is. ORCAS should carry range, active regime, visible count and current focus. Proposed as P8.D8.
Coordinate frame declared in the chrome Take it. Costs one line and makes a claim the project can stand behind. It also sits squarely inside the vault's own units-and-frames rule, which already requires every transform to name its frames in code — this puts the same discipline on screen.
Colour as an opt-in layer over a neutral default Take it. Neutral reads structure; colour reads classification. Making colour a mode rather than the default keeps both available.
Uppercase via text-transform, sentence case in source Take it. Free, and it keeps strings legible where they are authored.
Red for the destination marker Cannot take it. ORCAS reserves --critical #ff3b30 for conjunction alerts and nothing else. Focus and destination must be cyan. Specimen B does this — and it is the one place where the reference's surface is simply unavailable.
Curated labels only Reject. Verified: clicking a star within two pixels of its rendered dot produces no panel, no highlight and no camera move. The reference is a chart. ORCAS is an instrument — select any of 46,250 objects and get its real data. That contract is the differentiator, not a nice-to-have.
No collision test on labels Reject. Visible as a bug in every capture, at a dozen labels. ORCAS will have orders of magnitude more.
Uncapped device pixel ratio Reject. The reference renders 3.4 megapixels on an integrated GPU. ORCAS caps DPR at [1, 2], and for fill-bound point rendering that existing rule is the more careful one.

Motion, measured

The transition from the default view to Galactic was sampled every frame by reading the range value out of the DOM.

Property Measured Bearing on ORCAS
Duration to settle 893 ms, 17.72 pc → 178 pc Faster than the 1.5–2.5 s the Cosmic Scales brief proposes. Worth reconsidering: sub-second felt responsive, not rushed.
Frame rate during transition 47 fps sustained on Tier C With bloom on, at uncapped DPR, 53,836 points. A real Tier C number, not an estimate.
Curve Symmetric ease-in-out, close to smootherstep, with a longer settle tail Slow start, fastest near the midpoint, long soft landing. No linear segment anywhere.
Cuts None. Every preset is an eased flight. Matches the brief's P7.D4 exactly, and preserves the motion parallax that is the primary depth cue.

Caveat on the curve. What was sampled is the range readout, which is derived from camera distance — not the interpolation parameter itself. If the implementation interpolates camera position along a path and computes distance from it, the curve seen here is that motion viewed through a nonlinear lens. The duration and the frame rate are exact; the easing shape is indicative.
