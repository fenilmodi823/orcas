## 💬 Likely Q&A

Prepare for these — they follow directly from the paper's weak points:

1. **"How do you obtain the covariance matrices? TLEs don't publish them."**
   Honest answer: they're modelled/simulated for the reconstruction. This is the paper's main limitation and it is exactly why future work targets live phased-array radar feeds.

2. **"What is the asymptotic approximation you use for the P_c integral?"**
   Be ready to describe it, and to say plainly that the exact double integral is not tractable at 60 FPS.

3. **"How was the Random Forest trained and validated? What was the dataset?"**
   Features: semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, period, derived parameters, B* drag term. Importance weights: velocity 0.40, eccentricity 0.25, altitude 0.20, inclination 0.15.

4. **"AUC 0.94 versus 0.70 — on what test set, and how was it split?"**

5. **"Does 60 FPS hold on non-RTX hardware?"**
   The README validates on RTX 5060 / Ryzen 9. Say so honestly, and mention the `<PerformanceMonitor>` degradation path.

6. **"Isn't this just visualisation of a known event?"**
   The strongest counter: the replay is not a video. The same propagator runs on 2009 element sets, which is what makes it a validation rather than a demo.
